 Saw nothing, therefore, but good manners in the other, and with regard to the gentleman, there was such a hearty humour, an open, trustilering love of the Admiral's side, as could not influence Sir Walter, who had been flattened into his very best and most polished behaviour by his assurances of his being known, by report, to the Admiral, as a model of good breeding. Crofts were approved, terms, every thing, and every body was right, and Mr. Shepherd's clerks were set to work, to work without having a single preliminary.
