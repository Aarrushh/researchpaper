 When I have heard some slight indication of the course of events, I am able to guide myself by the thousands of other similar cases which occur to my memory. In this present instance I was forced to admit that the facts are, to the best of my belief, unique. The puffy client put out his chest with an appearance of little pride and a dirty and wrinkled newspaper from the inside pocket of his jacket. With his head thrust forward and the paper flattened out at his knee, I took a look at the man and endeavoured, after the fashion of his companion, to read the indications which I had seen.
