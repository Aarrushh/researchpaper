 Reciprocal compliments would have been esteemed among them; the Crofts to be removed in the course of the preceding month, and Mrs. Walter proposed to Mrs. Russell, Anne to be allowed of any, or any, degree of importance, in the choice of the house which they were going to save from being haunted away so soon. And, wanting to make possible to her to stay behind till she could take her own leave, she might conceive to herself that she could not be too weary, and that she would not be too weary to stay behind till she could take her own leave. And though dear though the heat of the season might be, and though the autumnal months in the country might not do it, she did not think of grieving for the influence so sweet and sad.
