 Emotion; he placed his hands before his eyes, and my voice quivered and failed me as I beheld between his fingers a groan burst from his heaving breast. I paused at length where he spoke, in broken accents: "Unhappy man! Do you share my madness? Have you drunk draught? Have you heard me? Let me reveal my tale, and you will dash the cup from your lips!" Such words, you may imagine, strongly excited my curiosity; but I was too much in love with the man to be moved by his words.
