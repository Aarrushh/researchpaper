 For my own part, I begin to love him, as a brother, and his constant sympathy and compassion. He must have been a noble creature in his better days, being attractive and amiable. I said in one of my letters, Margaret, I should find no friend on the wide ocean; yet I have found a friend who, before broken by misery, I should have been happy to possess as the other of my brother's journal shall continue my journal concerning the stranger.
