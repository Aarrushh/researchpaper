 To all they had to tell, I began to walk up and down near Briony Lodge once more, and to think " over my plan of campaign. " This Godfrey Norton was evidently an important factor in the matter. He was a lawyer. That was sounded ominous. What was the relation between them, and what the object of his repeated visits? Was she his client, his friend, or his mistress? I had the former, he had the latter, and if the latter, I probably transferred the photograph to his keeping.
