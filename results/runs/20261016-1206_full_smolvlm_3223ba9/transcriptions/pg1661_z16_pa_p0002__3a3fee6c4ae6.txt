 To all they had to tell, I began to walk up and down near Briony Lodge, and to think over my plan of campaign. "This Godfrey Norton was evidently an important factor in the matter. He was a lawyer. That sounded ominous. What was the relation between them, and what the object of his repeated visits? Was he his client, his friend, or his mistress? If the former, had he probably transferred the latter?
