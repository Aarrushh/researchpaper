She had never received from her more than outward attention, notice, beyond the observances of complaisance; had never succeeded in any point which she wanted to carry, against prejudice, in inclination. She had been repeatedly very earnest in getting to Anne included in the visit to London, sensibly open to the injustice and all the discredit of the selfish arrangements which shut her out, and on many lesser occasions endeavoured to give on many lesser occasions the advantage of her better judgement and experience; but always in vain.
