 Warmth with her at Kellynch Lodge, every danger was to be avoided; and it was, in fact, a change which must both do both health and spirits good. Anne had been too little seen from home, too little known. Her spirits were not high. A larger society would improve them. She wanted her to be better known. The undesirability of any other house in the same neighbourhood for Sir Walter was to be let. But this was a profound secret, not to be breathed beyond their own circle. Sir Walter could not have borne the degradation of being known to design letting his house at Kellynch.
