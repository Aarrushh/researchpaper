 Dear Margaret,

I said in no of my letters,
my dear Margaret,
that I have found in no friend on the wide ocean; yet I have found a man
before his spirit has been broken by misery;
I should have been happy to have possessed as the brother of my heart.

I shall continue my journal concerning the stranger at intervals, should I have any fresh incidents to record.

My affection for my guest increases every day.

My admiration and my pity to a distinguished degree.

How can I see so noble a creature destroyed by misery without feeling the most poignant grief?

He is so gentle, yet so wise; yet so.
