Lady Russell felt this break-up of the family exceedingingly. Their respectability was as dear to her as her own, and a daily intercourse had become precious by habit. It was painful to look upon their deserted grounds, and still worse to anticipate the hands that were to be laid upon them; and to escape out of the way when Admirals and Mrs Croft first arrived, she had determined to make her own according to their example, and Anne was set down at Uppercross Cottage, in the first stage of Lady Russell's journey.
