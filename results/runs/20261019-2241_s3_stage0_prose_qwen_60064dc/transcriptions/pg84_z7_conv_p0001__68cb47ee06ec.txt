I never beheld Strypho's utterly destroyed. Before this, I was not unacquainted with the more obvious laws of Electricity. On this occasion a man of great research in natural philosophy was with us, and, excited by this catastrophe, he entered on the explanation of a theory which he had formed on the subject of electricity and galvanism, which led him to the conclusion that the electricity of the body was a kind of emanation from the brain, and that the brain was the seat of the life or of the imagination; but by some faulty the overthrow of these men discredited me to pursue my accustomed studies. It seemed to me as if nothing would or could ever be known.
