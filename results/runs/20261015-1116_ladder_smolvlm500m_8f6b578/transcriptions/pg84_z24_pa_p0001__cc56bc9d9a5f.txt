 To Mrs. Saville, England. St. Petersburg, Dec. 11th, 17. You will rejoice to hear that the commencement of an enterprise which you have regarded with such evil forebodings, I arrived here yesterday, and my first task is to assure your sister of my welfare and increasing confidence in the success of my undertaking. I am already far north of London, and as I walk in the streets of this city, I am filled with the hope of the future.
