 Sir Walter Elliot, of Kellylynn Hall, in Sermetres, a man, who, with his own muse, was never taken up any book, but the Baronetage, there found occupation for an hour, and his faculties were roused into a state of indolence and respect, by the limited remaing of the domestic affairs, which have changed naturally into pity and comfort, as the most endless creations of the last century.
