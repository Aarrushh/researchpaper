As the minuteness of the parts formed a great hindrance to my speed, I resolved, contrary to my first intention, to make the being of a gigantic stature, that is to say, about eight feet in height, and proportionally large. After having spent some months in successfully collecting and having arranged my materials, I began. No one can conceive the variety of feelings which bore me onwards, like a hurricane, and the enthusiasmism which to me ideal bounds, which I should first break through, and to death pour a torrent of light into our dark world. A new species would bless me as its creator and source; many happy and excellent natures would owe their being to them being so completely as I should deserve them. Pursuing these reflections, I thought that if I could bestow animation upon lifeless matter, I might in process of time (although death I now find it impossible) to revive life where had apparently devoted the body to corruption.
