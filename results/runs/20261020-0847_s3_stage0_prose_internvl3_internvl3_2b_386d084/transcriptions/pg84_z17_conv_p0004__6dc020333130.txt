My limbs now tremble, and my eyes swim with the remembrance; but then a resistance and almost frantic impulse urged me forward; it was indeed but a passing trance, that only made stimulus me feel with renewed acuteness so soon as, the unnatural stimuli ceasing to operate, I had returned to my old habits; disturbed, with profane fingers, the tremendous secrets of the human frame. In a solitary chamber, or rather cell, at the house of the house, and apartments by a gallery and staircase, I kept my workshop of filthy creation; my eyeballs were starting from their sockets in attending to the details of my employment.
