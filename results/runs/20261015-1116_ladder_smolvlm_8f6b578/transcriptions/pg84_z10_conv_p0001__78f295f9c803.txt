 To Mrs. Saville, England, St. Peterburg, Dec. 11th., 17th.
I rejoice to hear that you have no disaster. I am glad to hear that you are walking in the success of your sister's welfare and increasing confidence in the success of your own.
I feel, too, that a cold northern breeze may be blowing upon you, but I feel it with delight. Do you understand this feeling? This breeze, which nivels me with delight, does not come from the north, but from the south.
