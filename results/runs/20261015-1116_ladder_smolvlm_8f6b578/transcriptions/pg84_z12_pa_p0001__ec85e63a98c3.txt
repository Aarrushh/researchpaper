 To Mrs. Saville, England, 11th, 17 - 17:
I rejoice to hear that no disaster has accompanied the commencement of an enterprise which you have regarded with such evil forebodings. I arrived here yesterday, and here is my task to assure you of my welfare and increasing confidence in the success of my undertaking. I am already far north of London, and as I walk in the streets of Newcastle, I feel a northern breeze play upon my cheeks, braces my nerves and fills me with delight.
