 To Mrs. Saville, England, Dec. 17th. - You will rejoice to hear that the commencement of the enterprise which you have undertaken has been accompanied with a concurrence of the most unqualified approbation and that the success of it is now certain and that the funds have been fully furnished. I have the honor to be, Sir, your affectionate sister and to assure you of my sincere and hearty confidence in the success of your undertaking and increasing welfare and increasing confidence in the success of it. I am already far north of London and as I walk in the streets of Petersburg I feel a breeze which has travelled from the north of Russia and which I feel in my cheeks, which I feel in my heart and which I feel in my soul.
