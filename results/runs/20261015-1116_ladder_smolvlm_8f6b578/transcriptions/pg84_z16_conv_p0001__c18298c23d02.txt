 To Mrs. Saville, England. St. Petersburg, Dec. 11th, 1767. I have arrived here yesterday, and my first task is to assure my dear sister of my welfare and increasing confidence in the success of my undertaking. I am already far north of London, and I walk in the streets of Petersburg, feeling a cold northern breeze fill my cheeks, which braces my nerves and fills me with delight. Do you understand this feeling? Towards which I am advancing, gives me a foretaste of icy climes, and my daydreams become more fervent and vivid. I try in vain to persuade you that the pole is the frost and desolation; it ever presents the frost and desolation.
