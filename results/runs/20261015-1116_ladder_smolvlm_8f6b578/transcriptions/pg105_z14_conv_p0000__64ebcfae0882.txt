 Sir Walter Elliot, of Kellynch Hall, in Somersetshire, was a man, who, like no other man, took up no book but the Baronetage, there he found occupation for an idle hour, and consolation in a distressed one; his faculties were roused into admiration and respect, by contemplating the unremarkable list of the earliest parents, arising from the almost limitless creation of the parents, which he turned over and over, and which, if every other leaf were powerless, he could read his own history with an interest that never failed. At the page which favoured him so well, he found the title of KELLYN HALL.
