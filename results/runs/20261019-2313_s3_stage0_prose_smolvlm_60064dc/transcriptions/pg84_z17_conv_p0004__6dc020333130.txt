 Slight ligaments are bound to prosperity or ruin. It seems to me as if it was almost miraculous change of inclination and will. The immediate suggestion of the guardian angel of my life, the last effort made by the spirit of preservation to avert the storm that was hanging in the stars and ready to envelop her victory. An unusual tranquility and gladness of soul which followed the relinquishing of my ancient and latterly tormenting studies. It was thus that I was taught to associate evil with their prosecution, happiness with their disregard. A strong spirit of good, but it was ineffectual. A potent Destiny, and her immutable laws had decreed her utter and terrible destruction.
