 The Admiral, as a model of good breeding, was known by his polished behaviour. His house and grounds were approved, and his furniture was approved. Crofts were approved, and terms, time, and every body were approved. Every body was right, and Mr. Shepherd's clerks were set to work, without there having been a single preliminary difference to modify. Sir Walter declared that "This inditement sheweth," without hesitation, that the Admiral was to be the best-looking sailor he had ever seen.
