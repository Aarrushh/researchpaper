 When she heard that the life of her favorite was menaced, she could no longer control her anxiety. Her watchful attentions triumphed over the sinister disfigurement of the distemper, but the consequences of this imprudence were fatal to her preserver. On her third day she mourned the most alarming symptoms, fever and looks of her medical attendants prognosticated the worst. On her deathbed, her fervent prayer was that she might die before her best women did. She joined the hands of her first love and Elizabeth, her children, her first love's firmest hopes of future happiness were placed on the prospect of her union. Elizabeth, her love, would supply her place to her younger children. She regretted that she was taken from them, but she regrets that she is not.
