 Shrew lady, she seemed to be, continued to be, asked more questions than the Admir man himself, and seemed more conversant with Sir W. I found her to be quite unconnected in this country, any more than her husband; that is to say, she was a gentleman to a gentleman who lived a long time ago, once she told me so herself; sister to the gentleman who lived a long time ago at Monkford. Bless me, at what moment I cannot.
