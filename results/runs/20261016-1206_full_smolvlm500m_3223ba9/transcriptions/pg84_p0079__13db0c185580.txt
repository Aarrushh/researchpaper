 Half fearful, with which I am preparing to depart, I am going to unexplored regions, to "the land of mist and snow," but I shall kill no albatross; therefore do not be alarmed for my safety or if I should come back as worn and weary as the "Ancient Mariner." You smile at me, but I will disclose a secret. I have often allusion, but I will disclose a secret. I have often allusion, but I will disclose a secret.
