 Half fearful, with which I am preparing to depart, I am going to unexplored regions, to "the land of mist and snow," but I shall kill no albatross; therefore do not be alarmed for my safety or if I should come back to you as worn and woeful as the "Ancient Mariner." You will smile at my allurement, but I will disclose a secret. I have often been to the sea, but I have never been to the "Ancient Mariner."
