 Half fearful, with which I am preparing to depart, am going unexplored regions, to "the land of mist and snow." But I shall kill no albatross, therefore do not be alarmed for my safety or if it should come back to you as worn and woeful as the "Ancient Mariner." You smile at my allurement, but I will disclose a secret. I have often been to sea, but I have never been to the "Ancient Mariner."
