 Chapter IV. Mr. Wentworth was not Mr. Wentworth, and he was not Mr. Wentworth. A few months more, and he was making himself a comrade in consequence of the action of St. Dominic, and he had not immediately come to St. Dominic for a home at a Norfolk house for a month. Anne, a remarkably young woman, with great intelligence, spirit, and brilliance, had a great deal of affection for Mr. Wentworth, and he had a great deal of affection for her. She was very handsome, modest, and gentle. He had a great deal of attraction on either side, and he had hardly anybody to love. The encounter of her lavish recommendations could not fail to fail.
