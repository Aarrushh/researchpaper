 Your love and kindness...
Your affectionate brother,
R. Walton Letter 2,
Saville, England,
Archangel, 28th March,
17th November,
How slowly the time passes,
encompassed by me,
frost and snow...
Yet a second step is
taken towards my enterprise.
I have hired a
vessel and am occupied in
collecting my sailors,
those whom I have.
