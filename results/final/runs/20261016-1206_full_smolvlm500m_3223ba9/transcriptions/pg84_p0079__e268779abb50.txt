 Half fearful, with which I am preparing to depart, regions, to unexplored, mists and snow, but shall kill all albatross; therefore not alarmed for my safety or if I should come back to you as worn and woeful as the Ancient Mariner. You will still smile at me, but I will disclose a secret. I have always been a wanderer, a wanderer of the sea, and I have never been able to find a place where I can be at peace.
