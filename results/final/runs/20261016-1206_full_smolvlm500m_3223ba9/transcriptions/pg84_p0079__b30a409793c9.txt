 Half fearful, with which I am preparing to depart, I am going to unexplored regions, to "the land of mist and snow". But shall kill no albatross; therefore do not be alarmed for my safety or if I should come back to you as worn and woeful as the "Ancient Mariner". You smile at my allurement, but I will disclose a secret. I have often been a wanderer, but I am not a wanderer now.
