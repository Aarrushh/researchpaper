 Archangel I shall depart for the latter town in a fortnight or three weeks, and my intention is to hire a ship, which I can easily be done by paying the insurance for the owner, and to engage as many sailors as I think necessary to a good number of those who are accustomed to the whale-fishing. I shall not intend to sail until the month of June; and when the month of June is over, I shall return to the latter town.
