 Half fearful, with which I am preparing to depart. I am going to unexplored regions, to "the land of mist and snow". But shall I kill all albatross; therefore do not be alarmed for my safety or if I should come back to you as worn and woeful as the "Ancient Mariner". You will smile at my allusion, but I will disclose a secret. I have often been a wanderer, and have wandered through many regions, but I shall not go to the "land of mist and snow" again.
