 Concentrated disposition, but with all ardor. I was smitten with a first kiss for the knowledge of the world. I was smitten with the following creations of the poets, surrounded by the sublime shapes of the Swiss-German landscape, the changes of the seasons, the calm of winter, and the life of turbulence. My Alps-nunfriend found ample scope for admiration and delight. While my companion contemplated the manifestations of things delighted in the world, I was a secret to divine research, curious, research to unravel the hidden laws of nature, akin to a raindrop, unformed to me, among the earliest summers of my seven years. Parents gave up entirely their wandering life and fixed themselves in their native second son's.
