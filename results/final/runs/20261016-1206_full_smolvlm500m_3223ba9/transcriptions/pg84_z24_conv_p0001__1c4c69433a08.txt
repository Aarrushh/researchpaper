 A passion-remembering what she had suffered, and how she had been relieved to act in turn the angel of the afflicted. During one of her walks in poverty, a valet noticed her as being singularly dismal, and the number of half-clothed children gathered about spoke of her poverty shape. When he visited, he found a peasant and his wife, who were bent down by care and labour, distributing a marmalade to hungry babies. Above all the rest, there appeared a different one which attracted her mother far above all the rest.
