 Sir Walter Elliot, of Kellynch Hall, in Somersetshire, was a man who for his own amusement, never took up any book but the Baronetage; there he found occupation for an idle hour, and consolation in a distressed one; there he roused faculties into admiration and respect, by contemplating the limited remnant of the last: there any unelcome sensations, arising from domestic affairs changed him naturally into pity and contempt at the almost endless recreations of the last.
