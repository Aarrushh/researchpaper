 To Sherlock Holmes she is always the woman. I have seldom heard him mention her under any other name. In his eyes she eclipses and predominates the whole of his sex. It was not that he felt any emotion akin to love for Irene Adler, all emotions, and that one particularly, were abhorred at his cold, precise but admirably balanced mind. I was, he took it, the most perfect reasoning and observing machine that the world had seen, as a lover he would place himself in a false position. He never spoke of the softer passions, save for a gibe and a smirn. He never spoke of the observer-excellent for drawing the veil from the motives and actions. But for the trained reasoner to admit such intrusions into his own delicate and finely adjusted temperament was to introduce a distracting factor which might throw a light on the whole of his character.
