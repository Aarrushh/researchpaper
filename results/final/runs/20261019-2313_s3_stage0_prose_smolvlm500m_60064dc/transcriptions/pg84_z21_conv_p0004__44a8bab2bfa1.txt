 Which could never even step within the threshold of real knowledge. My mind of doubt built upon the foundations of science as though it was built upon slights of light. Surely our souls are constructed, and such slight inclinations are bound to prosperity or ruin. When I look back, it seems almost miraculous the last effort made by the guardian angel of life to avert the storm that was then hanging in the stars and ready to envelop me. My victory was an unusual one, and the glistening of our souls followed the relishing of our ancient and lately tormenting studies. I was taught to associate evil with their prosecution, happiness with their punishment.
