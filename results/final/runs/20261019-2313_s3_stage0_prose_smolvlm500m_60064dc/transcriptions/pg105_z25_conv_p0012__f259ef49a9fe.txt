 Sir Walter Russell, being engaged to Bath Clay, was a most important and valuable assistant to the latter in all the business before her. Lady Russell was extremely sorry that a measure should have been resorted to at all, wondered, and feared; and the affront it afforded to Clay was a very sore thing. Anne was of none, a very sore thing, and became hardened to the affair as keenly as Lady Russell. With a great deal of quiet observation, and a knowledge which she often wished less of her father's, Clay often wished less of her father's.
