 My firmest hopes for future happiness were placed on the prospect of your union. This expectation will be the consolation of your father, Elizabeth. I regret that I have taken you from you, and I am not able to deny that I wish to be with you in another world. I have no thoughts of bereftness, but I am resigned to death and will not envy you. I have no feelings that are direst in the world, but that is the evil that presents itself to the soul. And I see that the brightness of a beloved eye can ever be part of our own, which I have departed for ever.
