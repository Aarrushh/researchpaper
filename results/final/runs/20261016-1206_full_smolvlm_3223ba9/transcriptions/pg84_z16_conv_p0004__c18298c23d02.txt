 "Unhappy man! Have you drunk also of my intoxicating draught? Hear me, let me reveal my tale, and dash the cup from your lips! Words, you may paroxysm, strongly excited your curiosity; but grief that seized the stranger overcame his weakened powers, and many hours of repose and tranquil conversation were necessary to restore his composure. Having quelled the violence of your feelings, he appeared to himself for being the slave of passion; and quelling the dark tyranny of despair, he led me again to converse concerning himself personally.
