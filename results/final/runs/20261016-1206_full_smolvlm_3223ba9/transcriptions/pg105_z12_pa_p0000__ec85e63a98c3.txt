 Warm months with her at Kellynch Lodge, every danger would be avoided; and it was in fact a change which must do both health and spirits good. Anne had been to little seen. Her spirits were seen. A larger society would improve them. The more known. The undesirability of other houses in the same neighborhood for Sir Walter was certainly much strengthened by one part, and by one material part, and by the scheme, which had been happily engrafted on the beginning. He was not only to quit his home, but to go to the next.
