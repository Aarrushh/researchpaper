Elizabeth could not conceive how such an absurd suspicion should occur to her, and indignantly answered for each party's perfectly knowing their situation. "Mrs. Clay," said she, warmly, "never forgets who she is, and as I am rather acquainted with her sentiments than you be, I can assure you, upon the subject of marriage, she particularly notices, and that she reprobates all inequality of condition and rank more strongly.
