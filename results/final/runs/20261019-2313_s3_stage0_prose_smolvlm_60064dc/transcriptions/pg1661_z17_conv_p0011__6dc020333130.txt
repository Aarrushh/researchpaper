 TO THE RED-HEADED LEAGUE:
On account of the bequest of the late Ezekiel Hopkins, of Lebanon, Pennsylvania, U.S.A., there is now another vacancy which entitles a member of the League to a salary of £ 4 a week for purely nominal services.
