 The last effort made by the spirit of preservation to avert a storm was when the victorious victory was announced by an unusual tranquillity and gladness of soul, which followed the relinquishing of the relinking of my confinement and lately of my spirit. It was thus that I was taught to associate evil with my prosperity, happiness with my disregard, and to feel the effort of my spirit to be of good, but that it was of evil.
