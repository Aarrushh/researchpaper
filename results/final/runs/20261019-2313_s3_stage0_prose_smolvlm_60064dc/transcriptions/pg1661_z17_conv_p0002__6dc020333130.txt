"You did, Doctor, but none the less I must come round to my view, otherwise I shall keep on piling fact upon fact until your reason breaks down under them and I acknowledge you to be right. You have heard me remark that the strangest and most unique things are very often connected not with the larger but with the smaller crimes, and occasionally, where there is room for doubt whether any positive crime has been committed. As far as I have to do with it, I have no doubt that the most serious crime is the crime of doubt.
