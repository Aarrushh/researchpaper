 Her smiles and spent them upon us. She forgot even her own regret in her endeavours to make us forget. The day of her departure at length arrived. She had spent the last evening with us. He had endeavoured to persuade her to permit him to accompany her to become a fellow student, but in vain. His father was a narrow-minded trader and saw idleness and ruin in the aspirations and ambition of his son. Henry deeply felt the misfortune of being debarred from a liberal education. He said little, but when he spoke she read in his kindling eye and in his animated glance a restrained but firm resolve not to be chained to the miserable details of commerce. We sat late. We could not tear ourselves away from each other nor persuade ourselves away from each other.
