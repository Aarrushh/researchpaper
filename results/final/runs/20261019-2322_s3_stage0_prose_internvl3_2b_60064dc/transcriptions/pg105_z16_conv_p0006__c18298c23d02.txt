Mr. and Mrs. Musgrove were a very good sort of people; friendly and hospitable, not much educated, and not at all elegant. They were a numerous family; but the only two grown up, excepting Charles, were Henrietta and Louisa, who twentie and two brought with them to Exeter all the usual stock of accomplishments, and were now like thousands of other young ladies, living to be fashionable, happy, and merry. Their dress had every spirit, their faces were prettily, their manners unembarrassed and pleasant; they were of consequence at home, and favourites abroad.
