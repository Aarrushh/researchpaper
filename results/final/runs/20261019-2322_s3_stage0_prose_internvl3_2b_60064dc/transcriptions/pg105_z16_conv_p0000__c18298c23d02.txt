That brother had been long removed from the country and being a sensible man, and moreover, a single man at the time, she had a fond dependence on no human creature's having heard it from him. The sister, Mary, had then been out of England, and never visited him; and, moreover, her own sister, Lady Russell, had been at school while it all occurred; and, never before, and never afterwards, had she been so much attached to any human creature's having heard it.
