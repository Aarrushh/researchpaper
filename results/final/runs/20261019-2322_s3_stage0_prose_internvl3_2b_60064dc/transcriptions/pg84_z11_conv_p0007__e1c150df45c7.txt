Thus a day memorable to me it became; I decided my future; I read with ardor, so full of zeal and discrimination these subjects, attended the lectures and cultivated the acquaintance of the men of science, and found myself in a state of information, combined, it is true, with a repulsive physiognomy and manners, but not less valuable. I was not tinged by dogmatism, and his instructions were given with an air of frankness and banished every idea of a path of knowledge and made the most abstruse inquiries clear and facilitate my apprehension. My application was first proceeded and soon became so ardent and eager that the stars often disappeared in the morning and was engaged, heart and soul, in the pursuit of some discoveries which I hope to make.
