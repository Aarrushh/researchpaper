Lady Russell felt this break-up of the family exceedingingly. Their respectability was as dear to her as her own, and a daily intercourse had become precious by habit. It was painful to look upon their deserted grounds, and still worse to anticipate the new hands they were to fall into; and to escape the solitude and the melancholy of so altered a village, and be a way when she arrived, she had determined to make her own absence from home begin when she must give up Anne.
