It was not splintered by the shock, but entirely reduced to thin ribbons of wood. I never beheld anything so utterly unacquainted with the more obvious laws of electricity. On this occasion a man of great research in natural philosophy was us, and we, excited, and exclaimed, "Oh, what a theory which he had formed on the subject of electricity and galvanism, which was at once new and astonishing to me."
