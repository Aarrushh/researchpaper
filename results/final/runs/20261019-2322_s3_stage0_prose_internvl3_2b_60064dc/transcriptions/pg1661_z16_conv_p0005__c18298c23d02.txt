"Tell me all about it," said I, showing me the advertisement yourself. "The League has a vacancy, and there is the address where you can see yourself apply for particulars. As far as I can make out, the League was founded by an American millionaire, Ezekial Hopkins, who was very peculiar. he had a great sympathy for all red-headed men; so, when he died, it was found that he had left his enormous fortune in the hands of trustees, with instructions to provide easy berths to men whose hair is of that colour.
