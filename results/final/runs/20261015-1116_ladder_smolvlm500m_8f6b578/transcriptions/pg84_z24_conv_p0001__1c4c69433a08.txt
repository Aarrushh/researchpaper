 To Mrs. Saville, England. St. Petersburg, Dec. 11th, I rejoice to hear that no disaster has accompanied the commencement of an enterprise which I have regarded with such evil forebodings. I have arrived yesterday, and my first task is to assure my dear sister of my welfare and increasing confidence in the success of my undertaking. I have already been far north of London, and I walk in the streets of Peterborough, where I have been a cold northern breeze pleases me. I fill my bosom with delight.
