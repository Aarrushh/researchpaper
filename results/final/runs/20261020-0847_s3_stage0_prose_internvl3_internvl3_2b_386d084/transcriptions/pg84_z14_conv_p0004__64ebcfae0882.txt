My limbs now tremble, and my eyes swim with the remembrance, but then a rest is restored and almost festive and amiable pursuit. It was indeed a passing trance, that only made me feel with renewed attention as soon as I operated, I had returned to my old habits. I collected bones from channel-houses and disturbed, with profane fingers, the tremendous flitch of human flesh. In the top of the house, and separated from all the other apartments by a gallery staircase, I kept my workshop of filthiness, my employment which did not do my nature any good. The dissecting room and the slaughter-house furnished of materials; and loathing from my occupation, whilst still urged on by an eagerness which perpetually increased, I brought my work near to a conclusion. The months summer passed, one pursuit.
